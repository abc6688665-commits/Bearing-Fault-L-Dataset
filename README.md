# Bearing-Fault-L-Dataset
L dataset contains five bearing health states.The test object was an SKF 6001 deep-groove ball bearing.
The experimental platform consists of data-acquisition software, an eight-channel data-acquisition card, a bearing test bench, acceleration sensors, and a power supply. The acquisition software was deployed on the host computer for real-time monitoring, display, and storage of vibration signals. The eight-channel acquisition card was used for synchronous acquisition and transmission of sensor signals. The bearing test bench provided a controllable mechanical rotation environment. Acceleration sensors were installed at the test positions to measure vibration responses during bearing operation, and the power supply provided stable electrical power for the test system.The dataset contains five bearing health states, including normal condition, outer-race fault, inner-race fault, rolling-element fault, and cage fault. Data were collected under three rotational speeds of 1200, 1500, and 1800 r/min, combined with no-load and 10% load conditions, resulting in six operating conditions in total. The load level was defined as a percentage of the rated torque of 5 N·m. The vibration signals were sampled at 102.4 kHz, with a sampling length of 32768 points for each sample. Repeated acquisitions were conducted for each bearing state under all operating conditions. The dataset features controllable operating conditions, complete fault categories, and a high sampling frequency, making it suitable for evaluating the recognition performance and cross-condition generalization ability of bearing fault diagnosis models under variable speed and load conditions.
